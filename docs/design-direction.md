# Coastal laboratory

A full-viewport, finite muddy coastal basin is the experience. The camera is oblique, ocean foreground, intertidal mangroves and dry land behind. Quiet pale blue-grey surroundings frame the water; no landing page. A compact white instrument panel occupies the right edge, a small experiment identity the upper left, and a shared A/B switch above the scene. A narrow lower readout exposes the four observed outcomes. Typography: Georgia for the modest title, system sans for controls, Menlo for measured values. Palette: open water #2f798a, shallow water #70a599, silt #8c795b, canopy #305a3b, instrument ink #243a40, background #dbe5e7. Orange erosion and blue deposition are reserved for scientific overlays. The signature is the actual moving water/bed boundary and visible stilt roots, never decorative charts.

GPU requirement: real-time spatial inspection of authoritative wave, bed and root data. Raw WebGPU shares the simulation device and storage buffers with rendering. UI remains DOM. Rendering is read-only. No camera animation on load. Mobile is a compact alternate control layout, although the supported performance target is the user's M2 MacBook.
